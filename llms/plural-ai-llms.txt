# Plural

> Plural is an AI platform built for private markets professionals across private debt, private equity, and banking. Its AI agents ingest and analyze complex financial documents and data-room sources to generate auditable, fully-referenced outputs — deal screeners, IC memos, term-sheet drafts, portfolio reports, and compliance documentation. Plural emphasizes EU data residency, end-to-end encryption, ISO 27001-aligned controls, and GDPR/DORA readiness, with no training on customer data.
